## Hide a session

- The close dialog of a session has a Hide button again. Hide takes the pane off the canvas and keeps its session running. The pane stays in the sidebar.
- Click a hidden pane in the sidebar, the Inbox or a notification to put it back on the canvas. It stays there after you leave focus mode.
- A hidden pane is not a tab in tab mode, and Tidy arranges only the panes on the canvas.
