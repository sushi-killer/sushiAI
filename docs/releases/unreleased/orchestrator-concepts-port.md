# The orchestrator, Inbox and Settings follow the new design

- The Orchestrator panel opens on Home: what needs you, what runs, what landed today. A narrower rail groups tasks (subtasks nest under their parent), and a composer at the bottom starts a task. ⌘N focuses it.
- Chat has a list of chats on the right, task cards in replies and agent messages in the same place. Brainstorm lives under Plan in its own fresh chat and fills a task draft as you talk; Plan lists drafts ready to start.
- A finished task shows its report and a stage timeline: click a stage to see what the agent did, how long it took and what it cost.
- New Improvements and Analytics views; Settings has a side menu and an Orchestration page with autonomy presets, routes, limits and budgets.
- The Inbox works across every project and host with or without the orchestrator: sessions waiting on you (including Claude Code and Codex questions, answerable from the Inbox), orchestrator questions, decisions and work to land, with a preview and keyboard triage. Clean up shows what it will close first.
- Mascot notices show the project, age, cost and review result, pick answers with 1–9 and can fold into a pill.
- On a remote host the panel shows a host selector, a setup checklist (with the rustup command when cargo is missing) and a preflight strip; a lost daemon is noticed within seconds.
- Plan keeps a Next/Later backlog with an autopilot switch; Brainstorm runs in its own chat and turns the conversation into a task draft; Analytics pages through weeks with deltas and a daily budget.
