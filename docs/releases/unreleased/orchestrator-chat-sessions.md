## Several orchestrator chats per project

- The Orchestrator panel keeps several chat sessions per project. **New chat** above the conversation starts an empty one that shares no context with the previous chat; each session is listed under the title of its first message, and clicking one brings its messages back.
- The trash icon on the current session clears it: its messages go, and the next reply starts without any earlier context.
- While the orchestrator is answering, New chat, switching and clearing are disabled until the reply is in; a click that races the start of a reply shows "the orchestrator is still answering".
- The existing orchestrator conversation of a project is kept as its first session.
