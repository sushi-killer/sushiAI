## Notices for panes you cannot see

- An agent that waits for you now raises a notice even when sushiAI is the focused window, as long as the waiting pane is not the one on screen. Before, a pane that waited while you worked in another pane never told you.
- A notice is skipped only when you are already looking at that pane.
- A pane that keeps waiting is reminded at 5, 10 and 20 minutes, then every 30 minutes, until you answer it or open it.
