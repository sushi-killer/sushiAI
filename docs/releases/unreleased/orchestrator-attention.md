# Orchestrator tasks that need you show up in the Dock, tray and Inbox

Tasks waiting on your answer, failed, waiting to land on a dirty checkout, or
stopped on their own now count in the Dock badge and the tray, and appear in
the Inbox under "Orchestrator". Click a row to open the task in the
Orchestrator panel. A task that fails, waits to land or stops on its own also
raises a notice. A waiting task now says "N questions for you" with the real
count.
