## A planner check that cannot pass no longer costs an owner question

- When the agent reports a criterion impossible and its only check was written by the planner, orchd settles it itself: a judge turn drops the criterion or rewrites its check from the agent's report, and the reason is recorded. Without a judge route orchd drops it with an overturnable assumption.
- Repo and owner checks still ask the owner.
- The planner brief now asks for a check that proves behaviour (a test that fails before and passes after), not an exact test name.
