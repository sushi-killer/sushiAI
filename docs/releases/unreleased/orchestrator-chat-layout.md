## Orchestrator panel looks and works like Chat

- The orchestrator chat now uses the same transcript and composer as Chat. A chip in the composer picks the orchestrator's harness and model; the choice is saved to Settings → Orchestration and kept when the panel is reopened.
- Task rows show a colored status pill (Running, Waiting, Done, Failed, Queued, Stopped) and, for a running or queued task, its attempt progress such as "attempt 2/5".
- **New task** in the task list creates a task straight from a one-line request, without going through the orchestrator chat. When the planner is turned off, the request becomes the task's title and goal.
